01 — about

I've spent my career so far finding the painful manual process nobody's fixed yet — and fixing it.

My background is engineering, but the thread through everything I've built is the same one product work runs on: find where a process is slow, manual, or error-prone, understand who's affected and why it hasn't been fixed, then build and ship something that actually gets used. A stakeholder-facing Excel report replaced by a live dashboard. A quality-metrics email nobody read turned into a plain-language summary people act on.

Being close to the engineering side means I can scope realistically, talk to developers without a translator, and tell the difference between a hard technical constraint and an assumption nobody's questioned. My time in customer support has sharpened the other half — reading a user's actual problem instead of the ticket they filed, and communicating a fix in terms that land.

I've also formalized the analytical and process side: a Deloitte Australia data analytics job simulation, a JIRA project management certification, and hands-on Power BI and Excel work for stakeholder reporting.
